# Tirzepatide Landscape Brief — Journal Club Prep

**Prepared for:** Friday journal club
**Drug:** Tirzepatide (LY3298176), a once-weekly GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist
**Primary reference trial:** NCT05822830 (SURMOUNT-5)

---

## 1. Headline Trial: SURMOUNT-5 (NCT05822830)

*A Phase 3b, Randomized Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide Compared to Semaglutide in Adults Who Have Obesity or Overweight With Weight Related Comorbidities*

| Field | Registry value (as of today) |
|---|---|
| **NCT ID** | NCT05822830 (Lilly study ID I8F-MC-GPHJ; UTN U1111-1283-5404) |
| **Acronym** | SURMOUNT-5 |
| **Sponsor** | Eli Lilly and Company (Industry) |
| **Phase** | Phase 3b (registry phase: Phase 3; randomized, parallel, open-label, treatment purpose) |
| **Current overall status** | **Completed** (status verified 2025-11; start 2023-04-21, primary completion & study completion 2024-11-13; results first posted 2025-11-26) |
| **Enrollment** | **751 participants (ACTUAL)** — 375 tirzepatide (15 mg or MTD) + 376 semaglutide (2.4 mg or MTD); all enrolled at U.S. sites |
| **Population** | Adults (18+) with BMI ≥30, or BMI ≥27 + ≥1 weight-related comorbidity (hypertension, dyslipidemia, OSA, CVD); **without** type 2 diabetes |
| **Design** | Parallel-group, randomized, open-label; ~74-week study (72-week treatment); subcutaneous once-weekly dosing with 4-week titration to max tolerated dose |
| **Primary outcome** | Percent change from baseline in body weight at Week 72 |
| **Key result** | NEJM 2025 (Aronne LJ et al., 393(1):26-36); ≥10% weight reduction in **87.7%** (tirzepatide) vs **66.7%** (semaglutide); LS mean difference −6.5% (95% CI −8.1 to −4.9) |
| **Safety** | Comparable tolerability; injection-site reactions rare with tirzepatide; GI metric profile (nausea, diarrhea, constipation, vomiting) consistent with GIP/GLP-1 class; 0 deaths in either arm; pooled IPD available via Vivli under DSA |

> **Registry takeaway:** The head-to-head vs semaglutide in obesity + comorbidities (no T2D) is now a **completed, results-posted Phase 3b** study with 751 enrolled participants, sponsored and run entirely by Eli Lilly at U.S. sites.

**Reading of the landscape:**
- **Phase 2 (80) is the largest active development bucket** — proof-of-concept expansion across obesity, MASH, OSA, HFpEF, and combinations indications.
- **Phase 3 (60) + Phase 4 (50) = 110 late-stage trials (~35% of the registry)**, reflecting tirzepatide's mature pipeline (SURMOUNT-1/2/3/4/5, SURMOUNT-OSA, SURMOUNT-MASH) plus post-marketing CV/renal outcome studies.
- **Phase 1 (41) + Early Phase 1 (6) = 47** early proof-of-efficacy studies (often obesity + T2D combo programs).
- **31 "N/A"** are non-interventional records (observational studies, registries) and **58 "Unknown"** lack a phase designation — together ~29% of the 314 don't map to a conventional phase bucket.

**Bottom line for the room:** The registry shows a **balanced, late-stage-heavy portfolio** — 131 Phase 2/3 studies plus 50 Phase 4 trials dominate, with 80 Phase 2 studies marking where the next wave of indications (MASH, HFpEF, combinations) is being de-risked now that SURMOUNT-5 has answered the head-to-head obesity question vs semaglutide.

## 3. Quick-Fire Numbers for the Slide

- **751 total enrolled (actual)** — 375 tirzepatide (374 randomized with ≥1 dose) vs 376 semaglutide (376 randomized with ≥1 dose)
- Week 72 weight change: **−20.2%** (tirzepatide) vs **−13.7%** (semaglutide); **−6.5 pp** difference (95% CI −8.1 to −4.9)
- ≥10% weight reduction: **87.7%** vs **66.7%**; ≥15%: **71.7%** vs **45.0%**; ≥20%: **55.0%** vs **31.1%**; ≥25%: **36.5%** vs **18.6%**; ≥30%: **23.0%** vs **8.2%**
- Waist circumference: **−18.4 cm** vs **−13.0 cm** (−5.4 cm difference; 95% CI −7.1 to −3.6)
- BMI change: **−8.5** vs **−6.0 kg/m²** (−2.6 kg/m² difference; 95% CI −3.2 to −1.9)
- 0 deaths in either arm; serious adverse events ~18% (tirzepatide) vs ~13% (semaglutide)
